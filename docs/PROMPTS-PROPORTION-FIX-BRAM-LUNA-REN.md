```
Keep EVERYTHING else identical to image 1: the same face and expression, hairstyle and hair color, skin tone, eye color, outfit design and colors, accessories, markings, art style, line weight, cel-shading, lighting, facing direction and standing pose. Both hands EMPTY (no weapon, shield, staff or held item). Isometric camera slightly looking down, three-quarter front view as in image 1.
Output: one character only, full body, nothing cropped, feet at the bottom center of a square canvas, plain flat bright green background (#00FF00), no ground, no cast shadow, no text, no frame.
```
